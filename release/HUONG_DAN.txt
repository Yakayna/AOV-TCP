CHE DO ONLINE 24/7 (GIU LOBBY) - QUAN TRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
Neu ban muon acc o trong lobby lien tuc (khong tat cmd):

1. Mo thu muc Final, nhay dup: LUQ_Online.cmd
2. Nhap tai khoan/mat khau nhu binh thuong.
3. Giai captcha neu Chrome hien ra.
4. Sau khi thay "SESSION CREATED + LOBBY DATA", tool tu chuyen sang
   che do giu lobby:
      [hold] ONLINE | 148.222.90.36:6629 frames=... big=...
   Cu ~90 giay no ping lai 1 lan de session khong bi server huy.
5. GIU CUA SO CMD MO 24/7 (may tinh phai bat). Dong cua so = dung.
   Muon dung chu dong: bam Ctrl+C trong cua so CMD.
6. Neu session bi rot (vd. mat mang), tool tu tao lai session.
   Neu token het han (sau nhieu ngay), tool se bao:
      "GOI Y: ... chay lai LUQ_Login.cmd"
   -> chi can chay lai LUQ_Online.cmd la xong.
7. Log giu lobby: Final\captures\hold_log.txt

LUU Y: che do nay KHONG can LDPlayer/game client, chi can internet
va cua so CMD con mo.

                         loi dan Ctrl+V bi mat ky tu)

   ("Verify you are human" dang checkbox/slider):
      -> HAY GIAI CAPTCHA do (click/kéo theo huong dan)
      -> KHONG dong cua so Chrome
      -> Cho toi da ~200 giay, tool se tu tiep tuc
   (Lan dau co the can giai captcha; cac lan sau cookie con han
